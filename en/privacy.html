Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026 · Beta

Summary

WEEKLINE beta has no sign-up and does not send your goals, schedule or records to our servers. Everything stays in your device’s browser storage.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or location.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Goals, weekly plans, daily schedules, check records, meeting and trip details, display settings
  • Where: your device’s browser storage (localStorage)
  • We cannot access this information. It is deleted when you clear browser data or remove the app.
  • Backup files you download are kept by you and are never sent to us.

3. Third-party services

  • Fonts: the app loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive standard connection data such as your IP address. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Hosting: the app files are served by GitHub Pages, which may process access logs. (GitHub Privacy Statement)

4. Notifications

If you turn on meeting alerts, the app shows your device’s own notifications for meeting prep and departure times while it is open. Alert content never leaves your device.

5. Children

Because the service collects no personal information, it does not check age.

6. Changes

If we add features that process personal information, such as account sync or payments, we will update this page and notify you in the app before they take effect.

7. Contact

Please contact us through the GitHub issues page.
