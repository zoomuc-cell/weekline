Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026 (previous version: October 4, 2026)

Summary

WEEKLINE has no sign-up and does not send your goals, schedule or records to our servers. Everything stays on your device (browser storage on the web, app storage in the phone app). PRO payments are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never receive payment details.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or location.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Goals, weekly plans, daily schedules, check records, meeting and trip details, display settings
  • Where: storage on your device (web: browser localStorage; phone app: the app’s own storage)
  • We cannot access this information. It is deleted when you clear browser data or remove the app.
  • Backup files you create are kept by you (in the phone app, saved wherever you choose in the share sheet) and are never sent to us.
  • The date of your last backup, the start of the PRO free trial and whether PRO is active are also stored only on your device.

3. Third-party services

  • Fonts: the app loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive standard connection data such as your IP address. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Hosting: the web version is served by GitHub Pages, which may process access logs. (GitHub Privacy Statement)
  • Payments (PRO): PRO purchases and subscriptions in the phone app are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your name or payment details; the app only checks the store's purchase list on your device to see whether PRO is active. Manage, cancel or request refunds in the store. (Apple Privacy Policy · Google Payments Privacy Notice)

4. Notifications

If you turn on meeting alerts, you get your device’s own notifications for meeting prep and departure times.

  • Phone app: upcoming alerts (up to 60) are scheduled with the operating system in advance, so they arrive even when the app is closed. On Android, turning on “Allow on-time alerts” makes them exact; otherwise they may arrive a few minutes late.
  • Web version: alerts show only while the app is open in your browser.
  • Alert content is scheduled and shown on your device only and never sent to us. You can turn alerts off any time in your device settings.

5. Children

Because the service collects no personal information, it does not check age.

6. Changes

If we add features that process personal information, such as account sync, we will update this page and notify you in the app before they take effect.
