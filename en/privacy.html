Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026 · Operated by AURA Technologies Inc.

Summary

WEEKLINE has no sign-up and does not send your goals, schedule or records to our servers. Your records are stored in the app or browser storage on your device.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or location.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Goals, weekly plans, daily schedules, check records, meeting and trip details, display settings, free-trial start date
  • Where: your device’s app or browser storage (localStorage)
  • We cannot access this information. It is deleted when you clear browser data or remove the app.
  • Backup files are kept by you. If you share one by mail, a drive or another service, that service’s policy applies. Backups are never sent to us.
  • If automatic backup is on for your Android device, your device settings may include app data in your Google account backup. We cannot access that backup.

3. Third-party services

  • Fonts: the app loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive standard connection data such as your IP address. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Hosting: the app files are served by GitHub Pages, which may process access logs. (GitHub Privacy Statement)
  • Payments (PRO): PRO purchases and subscriptions in the phone app are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your payment details; the app only checks the store's purchase list on your device to see whether PRO is active.

4. Notifications

If you allow alerts, your device’s own notifications remind you of meeting prep and departure times. In the phone app they arrive on time even when the app is closed; on the web they arrive while the page is open. Alert content is never sent to us.

5. Children

The service is not directed to children. Because it collects no personal information, it does not check age.

6. Changes

If we add features that process personal information, such as account sync, we will update this page and notify you in the app before they take effect.
