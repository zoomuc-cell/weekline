## English (en-US)

**App name**
WEEKLINE - Goals & Week Planner

**Short description**
Turn this year's goals into this week's big rocks and today's plan.

**Full description**
WEEKLINE breaks yearly and quarterly goals down into months, weeks and today.

■ From goals to today, on one line
· Write personal and work goals; WEEKLINE splits them by month, week and day and shows progress as a %.
· Pick three "big rocks" each week first, then fill the rest of your time.

■ Your day and your meetings
· Plan the day in time blocks and check off what's done.
· Find free time for meetings, get prep and departure alerts — on time even when the app is closed.
· Trips, daily and weekly memos, weekly report.

■ Your records stay on your phone
· No sign-up, no login. Everything is stored on the device and never sent to a server.
· Export a backup file to Drive or mail and restore it any time.

■ Easy to read
· Adjustable text size, dark mode, Korean and English.

■ PRO (meetings)
· Booking, rescheduling and trip prep are PRO features — free for the first 14 days.
· ₩2,900 a month · ₩19,900 a year · ₩39,900 lifetime

## 데이터 보안 (Data safety) 답변

- 앱이 사용자 데이터를 수집하거나 공유하나요? → **아니요** (모든 기록은 기기에만 저장, 서버 전송 없음)
- 광고 포함 → **아니요** · 인앱 구매 → **있음** (PRO 구독·평생, 결제는 Google Play가 처리 — 개발자가 받는 결제 데이터 없음)
- 콘텐츠 등급 설문 → 유틸리티/생산성, 폭력·성적 콘텐츠·사용자 간 소통·위치 공유 모두 **아니요** → 전체이용가
- 타겟 연령 → 18세 이상 (어린이 대상 아님)
- 정부 앱 / 금융 기능 / 건강 앱 → 아니요
- 권한 설명: 알림(미팅 알림), 정확한 알람(미팅 시각 알림 — '알람 및 리마인더'), 부팅 후 알림 복원
