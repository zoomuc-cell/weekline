2. Purpose and target audience
Agenda to Action (formerly WEEKLINE) is a goal and weekly planner for working adults, team leads and small-business owners. Users write yearly or quarterly goals (personal and work), and the app breaks them down into months, weeks and today's time blocks, showing progress as a percentage. Each week the user first picks a few "big rocks" (the most important tasks), then fills the rest of the time. It solves the gap between long-term goals and what actually gets done today, and it also plans meetings and business trips with alerts. All data stays on the device; there is no account and no server.

3. How to use the main features (no login, no account, no sample files needed)
- Launch the app. There is no sign-up or login. Data is stored only on the device.
- Bottom tabs: "목표" (Goals), "오늘" (Today), "미팅" (Meetings, the round + button in the middle), "일정" (Calendar), "이번 주" (This week). The English UI uses the same order.
- Goals: tap a goal to add or edit it; progress rolls up from the weekly and daily items. Today: add time blocks and tap the circle to check one off. This week: pick the week's big rocks and open the weekly report.
- Meetings (PRO): find a free time slot, book or reschedule a meeting, prepare a business trip, and turn on meeting alerts (iOS asks for notification permission). Alerts are local notifications.
- Paid features: WEEKLINE PRO. All PRO features are free for 14 days from the first launch (an in-app trial, not an App Store introductory offer). To see the in-app purchases at any time, open the Meetings tab and tap the link under the title "PRO 무료 체험 N일 남음 · PRO 보기" ("See PRO" in English). It can also be opened from the Goals tab > backup card > "WEEKLINE PRO". The PRO screen lists:
  - PRO Monthly (weekline_pro_monthly) and PRO Yearly (weekline_pro_yearly): auto-renewable subscriptions in the group "WEEKLINE PRO"
  - PRO Lifetime (weekline_pro_lifetime): non-consumable one-time purchase
  with prices from the App Store, the auto-renewal terms, Restore Purchases, Manage Subscription (after purchase), the Privacy Policy and Apple's standard Terms of Use (EULA). After a sandbox purchase the badge changes to "PRO 사용 중" and PRO stays unlocked after the trial ends.
- Backup: Goals tab > backup card > "백업 파일 받기" exports a JSON file through the share sheet; "백업에서 복원" imports it.
- The app has no account creation, so no account deletion is needed. There is no user-generated content shared with other users.

4. External services
- Apple StoreKit / App Store: in-app purchases, restore and subscription management. No other payment processor.
- Apple local notifications and the iOS share sheet (on-device).
- Google Fonts: web font files only; no personal data is sent.
- No analytics, no ads, no AI services, no login or authentication service, no own server.

5. Regional differences
The app works the same in every region where it is offered. There are no region-specific features or content. The interface is available in Korean and English.

6. Regulated industry / third-party material
Not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry and contains no protected third-party content.

## 2) 화면 녹화 순서 (TestFlight 테스터의 아이폰, 1~2분)
녹화하는 분께 그대로 보내 주세요.

1. 설정 › 제어 센터 › "화면 기록" 추가 → 제어 센터를 내려 녹화 시작 (3초 카운트)
2. 홈 화면에서 **WEEKLINE 아이콘을 눌러 앱 실행부터** 시작
3. 하단 탭을 차례로 누르기: 목표 → 오늘 → 일정 → 이번 주 (각 화면 2~3초)
4. 오늘 탭: **+ 또는 빈 시간**을 눌러 할 일 하나 추가 → 동그라미를 눌러 완료 체크
5. 가운데 **미팅(+)** 탭: 미팅 하나 추가 → "미팅 알림 켜기" 누르고 알림 허용
6. 미팅 화면 제목 아래 **"PRO 무료 체험 … · PRO 보기"** 누르기 → PRO 화면에서 월간·연간·평생 가격, 자동 갱신 안내, 구매 복원, 개인정보처리방침, 이용약관이 보이게 천천히 스크롤
7. **월간** 누르기 → 샌드박스 결제 창이 뜨는 것까지 (결제 완료되면 "PRO 사용 중" 표시까지)
8. **구매 복원** 한 번 누르기
9. 목표 탭 › 백업 카드 › "백업 파일 받기" → 공유 창이 뜨면 닫기 → 녹화 종료
